Security Services Guide

How to Write a Security Services CV & Cover Letter in Nigeria

What to include, which skills to highlight, and example achievement lines for a Security Services application — plus ready-to-edit templates.

What to include in your Security Services CV

For a Security Supervisor role, lead your professional summary with your years of experience and your single strongest result — recruiters in this field decide whether to keep reading within the first few seconds. Keep your title line close to how the job advert phrases it.

Skills recruiters look for

Access ControlPatrol ManagementCCTV MonitoringIncident ReportingTeam Supervision

Example achievement lines

Don't just list your duties — show the result. Here's the kind of specific, numbers-first bullet point that works well for this role:

  • Supervise a team of 18 security officers across 3 shifts
  • Reduced facility incidents by 40% through improved access control procedures

Certifications worth listing

  • Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC) Certified

What to include in your Security Services Cover Letter

Open by naming the exact role and, if possible, the company — for example, a strong opening line for this field reads something like: "I am writing to apply for the Security Supervisor role advertised on your careers page. I currently supervise 18 security officers across three shifts and reduced facility incidents by 40% through improved access control."

Use your second paragraph to connect one concrete achievement (ideally with a number, like the examples above) directly to what the employer is likely looking for. Close with a short, confident line inviting an interview — see the full cover letter guide for the paragraph-by-paragraph breakdown.
