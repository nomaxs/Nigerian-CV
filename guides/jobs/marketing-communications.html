Marketing & Communications Guide

How to Write a Marketing & Communications CV & Cover Letter in Nigeria

What to include, which skills to highlight, and example achievement lines for a Marketing & Communications application — plus ready-to-edit templates.

What to include in your Marketing & Communications CV

For a Marketing Communications Officer role, lead your professional summary with your years of experience and your single strongest result — recruiters in this field decide whether to keep reading within the first few seconds. Keep your title line close to how the job advert phrases it.

Skills recruiters look for

Brand CampaignsMedia RelationsContent StrategySocial Media ManagementCopywriting

Example achievement lines

Don't just list your duties — show the result. Here's the kind of specific, numbers-first bullet point that works well for this role:

  • Plan and execute integrated campaigns across social and traditional media
  • Manage relationships with 6 media and PR agencies

Certifications worth listing

  • Google Digital Marketing Certificate

What to include in your Marketing & Communications Cover Letter

Open by naming the exact role and, if possible, the company — for example, a strong opening line for this field reads something like: "I am writing to apply for the Marketing Communications Officer role advertised on your careers page. I currently plan integrated campaigns across social and traditional media, and manage relationships with six media and PR agencies."

Use your second paragraph to connect one concrete achievement (ideally with a number, like the examples above) directly to what the employer is likely looking for. Close with a short, confident line inviting an interview — see the full cover letter guide for the paragraph-by-paragraph breakdown.
