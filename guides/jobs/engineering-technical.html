Engineering (Technical) Guide

How to Write a Engineering (Technical) CV & Cover Letter in Nigeria

What to include, which skills to highlight, and example achievement lines for a Engineering (Technical) application — plus ready-to-edit templates.

What to include in your Engineering (Technical) CV

For a Petroleum / Production Engineer role, lead your professional summary with your years of experience and your single strongest result — recruiters in this field decide whether to keep reading within the first few seconds. Keep your title line close to how the job advert phrases it.

Skills recruiters look for

Field OperationsEquipment Maintenance PlanningHSE ComplianceTechnical ReportingTeam Supervision

Example achievement lines

Don't just list your duties — show the result. Here's the kind of specific, numbers-first bullet point that works well for this role:

  • Analyse field operations to create improvement plans
  • Supervise equipment maintenance across 3 active sites
  • Plan and forecast drilling operations for new fields and expansions

Certifications worth listing

  • HSE Level 3 Certification

What to include in your Engineering (Technical) Cover Letter

Open by naming the exact role and, if possible, the company — for example, a strong opening line for this field reads something like: "I am writing to express my interest in the Production Engineer role advertised on your careers portal. Over the past decade I have planned and supervised equipment maintenance across several active drilling sites, with a consistent focus on safety and uptime."

Use your second paragraph to connect one concrete achievement (ideally with a number, like the examples above) directly to what the employer is likely looking for. Close with a short, confident line inviting an interview — see the full cover letter guide for the paragraph-by-paragraph breakdown.
