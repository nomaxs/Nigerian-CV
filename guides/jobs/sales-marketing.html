Sales & Marketing Guide

How to Write a Sales & Marketing CV & Cover Letter in Nigeria

What to include, which skills to highlight, and example achievement lines for a Sales & Marketing application — plus ready-to-edit templates.

What to include in your Sales & Marketing CV

For a Sales & Marketing Executive role, lead your professional summary with your years of experience and your single strongest result — recruiters in this field decide whether to keep reading within the first few seconds. Keep your title line close to how the job advert phrases it.

Skills recruiters look for

Territory ManagementTrade MarketingNegotiationCRM SoftwareTeam Leadership

Example achievement lines

Don't just list your duties — show the result. Here's the kind of specific, numbers-first bullet point that works well for this role:

  • Grew territory revenue by 22% year-on-year across 40+ retail outlets
  • Onboarded 15 new distributor partners in Lagos and Ogun States
  • Lead a team of 4 field sales representatives

Certifications worth listing

  • Certified Sales Professional (NIMN)

What to include in your Sales & Marketing Cover Letter

Open by naming the exact role and, if possible, the company — for example, a strong opening line for this field reads something like: "I am writing to apply for the Territory Sales Manager role. Over the past five years I have grown FMCG distribution revenue by 22% year-on-year across more than forty retail outlets in South-West Nigeria."

Use your second paragraph to connect one concrete achievement (ideally with a number, like the examples above) directly to what the employer is likely looking for. Close with a short, confident line inviting an interview — see the full cover letter guide for the paragraph-by-paragraph breakdown.
