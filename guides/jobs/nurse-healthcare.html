Nurse / Healthcare Guide

How to Write a Nurse / Healthcare CV & Cover Letter in Nigeria

What to include, which skills to highlight, and example achievement lines for a Nurse / Healthcare application — plus ready-to-edit templates.

What to include in your Nurse / Healthcare CV

For a Registered Nurse role, lead your professional summary with your years of experience and your single strongest result — recruiters in this field decide whether to keep reading within the first few seconds. Keep your title line close to how the job advert phrases it.

Skills recruiters look for

Patient AssessmentMedication AdministrationWound CareElectronic Health RecordsEmergency Response

Example achievement lines

Don't just list your duties — show the result. Here's the kind of specific, numbers-first bullet point that works well for this role:

  • Monitor and record vital signs for up to 20 patients per shift
  • Administer medication in line with prescribed charts and safety protocols
  • Support 2 newly qualified nurses through hospital onboarding

Certifications worth listing

  • Registered Nurse — Nursing & Midwifery Council of Nigeria (NMCN)
  • Basic Life Support (BLS)

What to include in your Nurse / Healthcare Cover Letter

Open by naming the exact role and, if possible, the company — for example, a strong opening line for this field reads something like: "I am applying for the Registered Nurse position advertised on your careers page. I am a Nursing & Midwifery Council of Nigeria-registered nurse with four years of ward and outpatient experience at a teaching hospital."

Use your second paragraph to connect one concrete achievement (ideally with a number, like the examples above) directly to what the employer is likely looking for. Close with a short, confident line inviting an interview — see the full cover letter guide for the paragraph-by-paragraph breakdown.
