Construction & Trades Guide

How to Write a Construction & Trades CV & Cover Letter in Nigeria

What to include, which skills to highlight, and example achievement lines for a Construction & Trades application — plus ready-to-edit templates.

What to include in your Construction & Trades CV

For a Site Supervisor (Building Construction) role, lead your professional summary with your years of experience and your single strongest result — recruiters in this field decide whether to keep reading within the first few seconds. Keep your title line close to how the job advert phrases it.

Skills recruiters look for

Site SupervisionHealth & Safety ComplianceMaterial SchedulingTeam CoordinationReading Building Plans

Example achievement lines

Don't just list your duties — show the result. Here's the kind of specific, numbers-first bullet point that works well for this role:

  • Supervise up to 25 site workers across 2 concurrent building projects
  • Enforce site safety protocols, maintaining zero lost-time incidents in 3 years
  • Liaise with project engineers on material scheduling

Certifications worth listing

  • NIOSH-Certified Site Safety Officer

What to include in your Construction & Trades Cover Letter

Open by naming the exact role and, if possible, the company — for example, a strong opening line for this field reads something like: "I am applying for the Site Supervisor role advertised in your recent notice. I have eight years' experience overseeing residential and light-commercial builds, currently supervising up to 25 workers across two concurrent projects."

Use your second paragraph to connect one concrete achievement (ideally with a number, like the examples above) directly to what the employer is likely looking for. Close with a short, confident line inviting an interview — see the full cover letter guide for the paragraph-by-paragraph breakdown.
