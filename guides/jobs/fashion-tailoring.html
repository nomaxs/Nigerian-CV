Fashion & Tailoring Guide

How to Write a Fashion & Tailoring CV & Cover Letter in Nigeria

What to include, which skills to highlight, and example achievement lines for a Fashion & Tailoring application — plus ready-to-edit templates.

What to include in your Fashion & Tailoring CV

For a Fashion Designer / Tailor role, lead your professional summary with your years of experience and your single strongest result — recruiters in this field decide whether to keep reading within the first few seconds. Keep your title line close to how the job advert phrases it.

Skills recruiters look for

Pattern DraftingBridal Wear DesignFabric SourcingTeam SupervisionClient Consultation

Example achievement lines

Don't just list your duties — show the result. Here's the kind of specific, numbers-first bullet point that works well for this role:

  • Design and produce 15+ bespoke bridal and occasion outfits monthly
  • Manage a team of 4 tailors and 2 apprentices

Certifications worth listing

  • Fashion Design Certification, Kaduna Polytechnic

What to include in your Fashion & Tailoring Cover Letter

Open by naming the exact role and, if possible, the company — for example, a strong opening line for this field reads something like: "I am writing to apply for the Fashion Designer role advertised on your careers page. I currently run my own atelier producing over 15 bespoke bridal and occasion outfits monthly, leading a team of 4 tailors."

Use your second paragraph to connect one concrete achievement (ideally with a number, like the examples above) directly to what the employer is likely looking for. Close with a short, confident line inviting an interview — see the full cover letter guide for the paragraph-by-paragraph breakdown.
